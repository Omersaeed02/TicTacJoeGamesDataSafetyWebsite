Delete your Tic Tac Joe Games account

This page explains how to delete your Tic Tac Joe Games account and the data associated with it.

Developer: Tic Tac Joe Games Pty Ltd

Option 1

Delete in the game

  1. Open Tic Tac Joe Games on your device.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account and its data are deleted immediately.

Option 2

Request by email

Can't access the game? Email us from the address linked to your account, or include your username / player ID, and we'll delete it for you.

tictacjoegames@gmail.com

Request account deletion

Please tell us how you sign in (Google account or username/password) so we can find your account. Requests are processed within 30 days.

What data is deleted

  • Your game account (username / player ID)
  • Linked sign-in information (Google account link or username/password login)
  • Saved game progress, stats and other player data stored on our servers

What data may be kept

We do not keep your account data after deletion. Limited records may be retained only where required for legal, security or fraud-prevention reasons, and are deleted within 90 days.

Deleting your game account does not delete your Google account. To manage your Google account, visit myaccount.google.com.
